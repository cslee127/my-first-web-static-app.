  • Tours 6" closure (RC 39) — matches PCN PDP/25/15486 (Rectifiers EWS → ST Singapore) + PCN PDP/25/15489-15491 (TVS Protection FE+EWS → Singapore + China subcons)
  • Crolles 300 expansion to 14,000 wpw — matches PCN 15789 (STM32C011x +Crolles 300) + PCN 16131 (STM32H5xx +Crolles 300 / +HHGrace Wuxi)
  • Singapore mature-tech consolidation — matches multiple FE/EWS transfers from Tours into ST Singapore
  • Rousset full saturation — matches PCN 15305 (STM32F411/F412/F76/F77 → Rousset RS8F at M10/90nm) + PCN 15586 (MEMS ASIC: Crolles 8" → Rousset 8")

Key Takeaways

  • Reshaping ≠ shrinking: ST is concentrating capacity into 300mm Si + 200mm SiC + GaN. French/Italian footprint stays (and grows in 300mm), but legacy 150mm in Tours and Catania exits.
  • Singapore Ang Mo Kio = global 150mm consolidation hub — important for parts that previously came from European 150mm lines.
  • Tours not closing entirely — keeps 200mm Si + GaN epitaxy + new panel-level packaging mission. Only the 150mm activities transfer out.
  • 2026–2027 = transition risk window — workforce departures concentrated here; expect ongoing Reshaping-related PCN volume.
