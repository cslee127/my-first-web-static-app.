Supply-Chain Hardening & Proactive Dual-Sourcing

TI · MPS · NXP · Microchip · ST · Intel · Diodes · Eaton · Infineon · Macronix

~50+ PCNs High

What is happening

The single biggest theme of 2026. Manufacturers are simultaneously diversifying assembly, bumping, and wafer sources — not because anything broke today, but to pre-empt single-point-of-failure exposure after multi-year cascade lessons (Sharp K2, Tower Israel, Amkor Korea, Nexperia ATGD).

Strategy by vendor

  • TI — 6 PCNs adding MLA, CDAT, TIEM, FMX, TI Clark, TI Philippines as alternates to existing Asia base.
  • MPS — 12 PCNs alone, adding GTA Shanghai (wafer), SCS / HTJS / GREATEK / Unisem-MY / Forehope (bumping/assembly).
  • NXP — i.MX 8M Plus + i.MX 93 cut over from Amkor Korea to ASE-KH Taiwan; Cpk PASS, AECQ100RevG.
  • Microchip — KSZ9131 PHY now tri-sourced (ASE Chung-Li + Amkor Shanghai + MTAI Thailand).
  • Intel — Vietnam added to 3rd Gen Xeon Scalable + Xeon W (existing Malaysia, Costa Rica).
2026's PCN volume (186 in 5 months vs 27 in all of 2025) is the visible signature of industry-wide supply chain hardening. Most notices are running changes — but they all need triage.
Advantech action: spot-check Cpk attachments, file all in Redmine, apply JEDEC J-STD-046 30-day tacit acceptance unless QA flags a critical part.

Au→Cu Wire & Alloy-42→Cu Leadframe Industry Migration

Diodes · Nexperia · NoVoSense · Micron NAND TSOP · Vishay optocoupler

12 PCNs Medium

Why now

Gold price + density + bond-strength engineering — multiple vendors are simultaneously migrating to Cu (or Cu-alloy) wire and Cu leadframes. The change is mostly transparent at the application level but introduces marking changes and re-qualification needs.

Notable changes

  • Diodes PCN-2798 — PdCu wire in CAT assembly
  • Nexperia CN-202512001F — SOT457 Cu wire 23μm + 50μm + 8" wafer + BSM + die layout
  • Micron PCN 36318 — 70s NAND TSOP: Alloy-42 → Cu leadframe + OSE Kaohsiung
  • NoVoSense NSI82xx — TFME Hefei + HDS PPF→PSJ Cu-Ag + Au→Cu + marking var code
  • Vishay PCN-OPT-1484 — VOM617A/VOM618A bond wire diameter reduction (BSOB)
Advantech action: Cu wire ≠ Au wire bend behavior. PCB assemblers should re-verify ATE board life cycle and check for any Au-specific datasheet caveat.
